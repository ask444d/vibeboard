# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in VibeBoard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards
- Be kind and collaborative. VibeBoard is local-first, not Jira — keep it light.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue with label `conduct` or emailing `conduct@vibeboard.dev`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

Enforcement may include warning, temporary or permanent ban.
